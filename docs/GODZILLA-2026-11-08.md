## Holes, worst first

### 1. Back pressed while the camera flies in to the console: the card opens anyway, and closing it leaves the site
- Steps: open the site from another page, click the console, press the browser's Back within about half a second.
- What happens: the address loses `#portfolio`, but the flight lands and the player card opens anyway. Its close (or
  Esc) then calls Back again, which leaves carlbahner.com for the page before. Seen twice on the console. On the drum the
  card did not open (timing).
- Why: `popstate` with no window open yet (`open` is set only when the flight lands) does nothing, so the flight lands
  and `show()` runs; `pushed` is still true, so closing calls `history.back()`.

### 2. A contact message with nothing in it sends, and says HOLY MOLY
- Steps: open CONTACT, type only spaces in First Name, Last Name and Message, a real email, press Submit (or Enter in
  the email box).
- What happens: it sends (name blank, message a row of spaces) and the toast says "HOLY MOLY, it sent!". Seen twice,
  once by Submit and once by Enter. Carl gets an empty message; the visitor thinks they wrote to him.
- Why: the boxes are `required`, and the browser counts spaces as filled in.
